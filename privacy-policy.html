In short

BeAlive works on your phone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your reminders, habits, photos, the apps and websites you block and your statistics stay on your device — we can't see them. The app connects to the internet only for BeAlive Premium: to Google Play, which handles payment, and to RevenueCat, which checks whether your subscription is active. We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

What stays on your phone

To work, BeAlive keeps the following only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your reminders, repeating reminders and habits, including photos you attach;
  • the apps and websites you chose to block, your block schedules and strict mode settings;
  • your settings and the optional nickname BeAlive greets you with;
  • simple usage statistics: how long you spend in blocked apps, how often you open them, the reason you pick when you do, and how often you close the pause screen instead.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, so we don't collect it and can't see it. It is deleted when you use Settings › Reset app or uninstall BeAlive.

The accessibility service

If you turn it on, BeAlive uses Android's accessibility service only to notice which app is in front — and, only if you block websites, the address shown in a supported browser's address bar — so it can show its pause screen when you open something you chose to block. It never reads page content, your messages, passwords or anything you type. The check happens on your phone in the moment; nothing is stored or sent. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings › Accessibility.

Premium and purchases

Google Play. BeAlive Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment and your Google account details under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your card or account details. Google Play's billing component may also send Google diagnostic information about purchases, and the optional rating prompt in the app is shown by Google Play.

RevenueCat. To know whether Premium is active, BeAlive uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as our processor. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore Premium, it sends RevenueCat:

  • a random, anonymous ID created on your phone (not linked to your name or email);
  • purchase and subscription data: the product, purchase token, order ID, dates, price, currency and status;
  • basic device and app information: device model, Android version, app version, language and store country;
  • your IP address, which any server sees when your phone connects to it.

BeAlive turns off RevenueCat's collection of device and advertising identifiers and doesn't use the advertising ID at all. Your reminders, apps, websites, photos and statistics are never sent.

Purpose and legal basis: providing the Premium you bought — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Transfers outside the EU: RevenueCat processes data in the United States. The transfer is covered by RevenueCat's data processing agreement, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (module 2, controller to processor).

Optional connections

Each of these works only if you use it, and Android asks for your permission first where one is needed.

  • Microphone and dictation — only while you dictate a reminder. Your phone's speech recognizer turns your words into text. When the phone can do that offline, nothing leaves it; otherwise your phone's speech service (for example Google's) processes the audio under its own privacy policy, and BeAlive tells you before the first time. BeAlive never records or keeps audio.
  • Share to BeAlive — text you share from another app only fills in a new reminder for you to check. It stays on your phone.
  • Photos and camera — a photo is picked with Android's photo picker or taken with your camera app. BeAlive only gets that one photo and keeps a copy in its private storage. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Notifications — to remind you of your tasks, show the session timer, the weekly recap and, during a free trial, a heads-up two days before it ends.
  • Installed apps — BeAlive reads the names and icons of your apps on the device so you can choose which ones to block.

This version of BeAlive has no calendar, health or cloud AI connections. If we add any, this policy will be updated before they're available.

Backups

If you've turned on Android's own backup, Android may include BeAlive's data in your device backup (for example in your Google account) so it can be restored on a new phone. That's handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

You can also save a backup file yourself (Settings › Back up to a file). It contains your BeAlive data, photos included, and is stored only where you choose. We never receive it.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

BeAlive contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, doesn't use the advertising ID, doesn't build profiles and doesn't sell or share your data. The only outside services it talks to are Google Play and RevenueCat, as described above.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it, reset the app or uninstall it.
  • RevenueCat: purchase records are kept for as long as needed to provide Premium and to meet our legal and accounting obligations (accounting and tax records for up to 10 years, as Czech law requires), then deleted.
  • Google Play and GitHub: under their own retention policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to a supervisory authority — in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz), or the authority where you live.

Most of this you can do right in the app: Settings › Back up to a file gives you a copy of everything, and Settings › Reset app erases it. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") so we can find them; we'll reply within one month.

Children

BeAlive is not directed at children under 13. The app doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 has bought Premium, contact us and we'll help delete the related purchase records.
