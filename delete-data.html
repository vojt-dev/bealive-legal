In short

BeAlive, the app by Vojta Skvan, has no accounts. Almost everything you put into it stays on your phone, so you can delete it yourself in a few seconds. The only data that ever leaves your phone is the record of a Premium purchase.

1. Delete everything on your phone

  1. Open BeAlive and go to Settings › Reset app, then confirm. Or
  2. uninstall BeAlive. Android then erases all of its data.

This deletes your reminders, habits, photos, blocked apps and websites, schedules, statistics and settings. Backup files you saved yourself (Settings › Back up to a file) stay where you saved them. Delete them in your Files app if you want them gone too.

2. Ask us to delete your purchase records

If you bought BeAlive Premium, our payment service RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase under a random ID (not your name or email). To have it deleted:

  1. Email BeAliveSupport@gmail.com with the subject Delete my BeAlive data.
  2. Include your Google Play order number. It starts with "GPA." and is in your Google Play receipt email, or in the Play Store under Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history.

We delete the records and confirm within one month.

What may be kept

Accounting and tax records of a purchase (amount, date and order number) are kept for up to 10 years, as Czech law requires, and then deleted. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchase under Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the Play Store under Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
